Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Early access registrations

When you join the INKNET early access list, we collect your name, telephone number, whether you are a tattoo artist or client, the date of your submission and your optional SMS marketing choice.

How we use your information

We use this information to administer early access interest and manage launch communications. Promotional SMS messages will only be sent where you have actively opted in. Joining the early access list does not require promotional SMS consent.

Storage and service providers

Registration information is stored using Supabase. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to help prevent automated submissions and may process technical information as part of its security checks.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your early access details, or withdraw SMS marketing consent, by emailing ombratatt@gmail.com. Any promotional SMS we send will include an opt-out method.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ombratatt@gmail.com.
