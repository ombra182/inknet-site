Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Early access registrations

When you join the INKNET email launch list, we collect your name, email address, whether you are a tattoo artist or client, the date of registration and your consent to receive launch updates. Previous registrations may also contain telephone numbers and optional SMS consent records.

How we use your information

We use this information to manage the INKNET Early Access list and send the launch news and updates you agreed to receive. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Storage and service providers

Registration information is stored using Supabase. Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent automated submissions and may process technical information. We intend to use Resend to manage and deliver launch emails.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your early access details, or withdraw email or SMS marketing consent, by emailing ombratatt@gmail.com. Marketing emails will include an unsubscribe method.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ombratatt@gmail.com.
